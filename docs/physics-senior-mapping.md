## 2. Mapping Curriculum-Standard Modules to Textbook Volumes and Chapters

The curriculum standard divides the compulsory curriculum into three modules—Compulsory 1, 2, and 3—and the selective-compulsory curriculum into three modules as well. Textbook volumes correspond one-to-one with the modules, but their chapter divisions do not align exactly with the standard's topics:

| Curriculum module | Curriculum-standard topics | Textbook | Chapters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compulsory 1 | Kinematics and physical models; interactions and laws of motion | Compulsory Volume 1 | Chapters 1–4 |
| Compulsory 2 | Mechanical energy and its conservation; curvilinear motion and the law of universal gravitation; limitations of Newtonian mechanics and an introduction to relativity | Compulsory Volume 2 | Chapters 5–8 (relativity is in Section 5 of Chapter 7) |
| Compulsory 3 | Electrostatic fields; circuits and their applications; introduction to electromagnetic fields and electromagnetic waves; energy and sustainable development | Compulsory Volume 3 | Chapters 9–13 (energy is in Section 4 of Chapter 12) |
| Selective Compulsory 1 | Momentum and the law of conservation of momentum; mechanical oscillations and mechanical waves; light and its applications | Selective Compulsory Volume 1 | Chapters 1–4 |
| Selective Compulsory 2 | Magnetic fields; electromagnetic induction and its applications; electromagnetic oscillations and electromagnetic waves; sensors | Selective Compulsory Volume 2 | Chapters 1–5 |
| Selective Compulsory 3 | Solids, liquids, and gases; laws of thermodynamics; atoms and atomic nuclei; wave–particle duality | Selective Compulsory Volume 3 | Chapters 1–5 (the curriculum standard places molecular kinetic theory under "solids, liquids, and gases"; the textbook combines atomic structure and wave–particle duality in Chapter 4) |

## 6. Known Limitations

- The curriculum-standard text was taken from the text layer of the publisher's PDF; it has not been checked word for word against the printed copy or the Ministry of Education's original document.
- The textbook tables of contents were taken from 2019-edition table-of-contents pages; copyright pages for the 2024–2025 printings have not been checked volume by volume.
- The curriculum standard's "Examples" and "Activity Suggestions" are not included; Electives 1–3 are not included.
- Knowledge-point granularity is one section or one major concept/experiment within a section; it does not correspond to every subheading in the textbook.
- Questions were written by AI and then independently reviewed and recalculated one by one in a separate pass; numerical problems and multiple-choice questions containing numerical values are automatically checked by a program, while purely conceptual multiple-choice questions are not automatically checked. No teachers or curriculum experts have reviewed them.
